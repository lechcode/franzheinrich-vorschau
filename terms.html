3. What I offer and what I do not

I support you in getting to know your feelings, emotions, shame and inner parts, and in building a new relationship with them. For this I work with, among other things, Emotional Release, breathwork and psychosomatic body work, guided visualizations, conversations, inner parts (inspired by IFS), shadow and process work, embodiment and experiments for your everyday life. What exactly is included in your offer (type and number of sessions, duration, WhatsApp support, experiments, audios and accompanying material, price) is stated in your offer description.

My work is self-exploration and accompaniment. I am not a doctor, not a psychotherapist and not an alternative practitioner (Heilpraktiker). I do not practise medicine, I make no diagnoses and I make no promises of healing. I do not treat physical or mental illnesses. My support does not replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment, but it can complement it. If you are in treatment, please continue it, and do not stop any therapy or medication because of our work. Please have physical or mental complaints checked by a doctor and/or psychotherapist.

All sessions take place live with me, online by video or in person. Recordings, audios and PDFs are only a reminder and an addition. They do not replace a session and do not teach learning content.

I owe you careful support, but not a specific result. What changes is different for every person.

7. If I have to cancel an appointment

If I have to cancel a session because of illness, a family emergency, force majeure (e.g. storm, flood, power or internet outage), or because I do not feel well enough to hold the space safely for you, I will tell you as early as possible. The session will be credited to you, and we will make it up, as a replacement appointment or in another form that works for you. This causes you no costs. Further statutory rights remain unaffected.

8. If I end the support

I can end our work together for good cause. This does not happen lightly and never without a conversation with you. Good cause is, for example, if you violate the boundaries of others, come to a session under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or if further support cannot be justified. Statutory rights of termination remain unaffected for both of us.

9. Your health and your safety

Emotional Release and intensive breath and body work can trigger strong feelings, body reactions and memories. So that we can work safely:

  • You may say stop at any time, end an exercise, or ask for more or less intensity.
  • Before our first session, you fill in my questionnaire completely and honestly, including the questions about your health, and you tell me if anything changes. This way I can adapt the work to you: depending on the situation, I leave out certain methods (e.g. intensive breathing), we only start after a medical check, or I refer you to other professionals.
  • If I notice that you also need medical or psychotherapeutic support, I will tell you openly and help you find it. Our work continues as long as it is good for you and can be justified.
  • In the questionnaire, you name an emergency contact person for me. If I can no longer reach you during or right after an intensive session, I may inform them, but only with your name and the request that they get in touch with you.
  • In in-person sessions, I only touch you if you have agreed beforehand. I ask anew every time. You can take back your yes at any time without giving a reason.
  • After an Emotional Release session, plan time for yourself and do not drive a car right afterwards. Something may also still come up in the days after. Feel free to get in touch with me then.

10. Availability between sessions

I usually answer messages by WhatsApp or email on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays, unless we have agreed otherwise. If I am offline, e.g. on holiday, at retreats or on days off, it can take longer.

I am not a crisis service and not available around the clock, not even by WhatsApp. If your life is in acute danger, please call 112 (EU) or the local emergency number immediately. To talk, you can reach the Telefonseelsorge (telephone counselling service) in Germany around the clock (0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222, Germany). You can reach the medical on-call service at 116 117 (Germany).

12. Confidentiality

What you entrust to me, I treat as confidential. I do not pass anything on without your consent. Exceptions: I am legally required to (e.g. in court, because I have no right to refuse to testify), or there is acute danger to your life or to the life or health of others. To be able to support you as well as possible, I sometimes discuss cases in supervision or with colleagues. This always happens without your name and without details by which you could be recognized.

You may share your own experiences with anyone you like.

13. Data protection, recordings and AI

How I handle your data, which services I use and whether data is processed outside the EU is stated in my privacy policy on franzheinrich.com.

I need your health information from the questionnaire so that we can work safely. I only use it with your explicit consent and only for that purpose. Without this information, I cannot carry out the support for safety reasons. Recordings of our sessions and evaluations with AI tools are voluntary. Our support takes place without them as well.

You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future. For recordings and AI, this has no consequences for our work together. If you withdraw consent to your health information, we will discuss together which form of support is then still possible.

I delete recordings and AI summaries at the latest 2 years after our support ends, earlier if you wish. After that, I only use insights from our work in a way that nobody can recognize you from them, to further develop my work.

14. Testimonials

I only publish your story, your process or your feedback if you explicitly agree, and only the way you want it: with your full name, with your first name only or anonymously, with or without a picture. You get to read the text beforehand. You can withdraw your consent at any time for the future. I will then take the post down from my channels within 14 days. Copies that others have already saved (e.g. screenshots) I cannot take back.

You will receive the withdrawal information and the model withdrawal form by email as a PDF before you say yes, and once more with my confirmation. If we conclude the contract in person, e.g. at a retreat, you will receive the documents on paper or, if you agree, by email. I only start before the 14 days have passed if you explicitly ask me to and confirm that your right of withdrawal ends as soon as I have fully provided the service. You do this in writing, e.g. in the questionnaire or by email. If you withdraw after that, you pay the share that has already been provided up to your withdrawal. The details are in the withdrawal information with model withdrawal form: franzheinrich.com/widerruf
