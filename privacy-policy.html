Privacy Policy

The German version of this privacy policy is legally binding. This English version is provided for your convenience. Deutsche Fassung: Datenschutz

It matters to me that you feel safe with me — on this website just as much as in our work together. That’s why I collect as little data as possible: this website sets no cookies and uses no tracking and no ads. Here’s what still happens with your data and what rights you have.

1. Privacy at a glance

What is personal data?

Personal data is any data that can identify you personally, for example your name, your email address or your IP address.

Who is responsible?

I, Franz Heinrich, am responsible for processing your data. You’ll find my contact details under “Controller”.

How do I collect your data?

On the one hand, you give me data yourself, for example when you write to me on WhatsApp or by email, or fill in one of my questionnaires. On the other hand, technical data is collected automatically when you visit this website, for example your browser, your operating system or the time of your visit. This is necessary so the website can be shown to you.

What do I use your data for?

To make this website work properly, to answer your enquiries, to prepare and carry out our work together — and, if you agree, to send you emails about retreats, my community and new offers. I don’t analyse your behaviour on this website.

What are your rights?

You can ask free of charge at any time what data I store about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, withdraw a consent for the future and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. More under “Your rights”.

2. Hosting

External hosting by GitHub

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the date and time of your visit, the page you visit, your browser and your operating system. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure, fast and accessible website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, on the basis of which the EU Commission has determined an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR). More in GitHub’s privacy statement.

4. Data collection on this website

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising services. That’s why there’s no cookie banner. The fonts are stored on the same server as the site, so no request goes to Google or other font providers.

When you switch the language, your browser remembers your choice (German or English) in local storage (key fh-lang). This stays on your device and isn’t sent to me. It’s necessary so the site is shown in the language you chose (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it anytime in your browser settings.

Server log files

The host of this website automatically collects and stores information in so-called server log files, which your browser sends automatically: browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, host name of the accessing computer, time of the request and IP address. This data isn’t combined with other data sources. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a technically error-free website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. Your rights

You have the right at any time to free information about your stored data, its origin, recipients and the purpose of processing (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20).

Withdrawing your consent: Many processing operations are only possible with your explicit consent. You can withdraw any consent you’ve given at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Anything that happened before remains lawful.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where I process data on the basis of my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. I will then no longer process your data, unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If I use your data for direct marketing, you can object to this at any time, and I will no longer use it for that purpose.

Right to restriction: You can ask me to restrict the processing of your data, for example while you dispute its accuracy, if the processing was unlawful and you prefer restriction to erasure, if you need the data for legal claims, or while it’s being checked after an objection whose interests prevail.

For all of this, just send me an email at info@franzheinrich.com.

Right to complain: You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the country where you live or work. The authority responsible for me is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
